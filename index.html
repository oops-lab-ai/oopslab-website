03 · The instinct

“ The interesting work isn’t in generating new data — it’s in making the data we already have legible.

Crime stats sit in 800+ municipal portals. Neighborhood risk sits in private vendors at $50K contracts. USB telemetry sits behind closed firmware. RFID location sits behind enterprise sales cycles. Every one of these is solved — for somebody else, at the wrong price, behind the wrong contract.

We pick problems where the access layer is the bottleneck and rebuild it for whoever’s being left out: developers, small operators, AI agents.

— Ian & Alex
